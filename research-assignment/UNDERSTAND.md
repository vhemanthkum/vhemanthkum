## Phase 2: How I'll Score Each Company

### The 6 Criteria Framework:

| Criterion | What I Check | Evidence I Look For |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **C1: Manufacturer** | Do they actually make products? | Website mentions facility, plant, manufacturing, production |
| **C2: India-based** | Is Hyderabad operational hub? | HQ in Hyderabad OR R&D/manufacturing in Hyderabad |
| **C3: Differentiated** | Specialty or commodity? | Patents, DSIR-recognized, USFDA approved, regulatory certifications |
| **C4: Technical DM** | Founder has tech background? | PhD, IIT/NIT/IISc degree, ex-ISRO/DRDO, engineering background |
| **C5: Tailwind** | Is sector growing? | PLI scheme, Make-in-India, China+1, export focus, government support |
| **C6: Growth** | Is company actively expanding? | Recent hiring, new facilities, certifications, press updates, active website |

**Scoring:** Weak / Moderate / Strong (with specific evidence for each)

## Phase 3: Red Flags I'll Watch For (Auto-Disqualify)

❌ **Traders/Distributors** — "We distribute X products"  
❌ **Service Companies** — CROs, testing labs, analytical services  
❌ **Generic Pharma** — Bulk APIs, branded generics, vaccines  
❌ **PE-Controlled** — Private equity owned, not founder-driven  
❌ **Revenue > Rs.500Cr** — Too large for our segment  
❌ **Stale Websites** — No updates since 2021 or earlier  
❌ **No Real Activity** — Last press release 5+ years ago

## Research Process (Step-by-Step What I'm Doing)

### Step 1: Build Initial Universe (Days 1-2)

**Goal:** Find ~100-120 potential companies in Hyderabad, Basket A

**Activities:**

**Output:** Spreadsheet with ~100-120 companies to investigate

### Step 2: Website Investigation (Days 2-3)

**Goal:** Visit each company's website, collect info on 6 criteria

**Activities:**
- [ ] For each company: visit homepage + about + products + team + news
- [ ] Note if they're actually a manufacturer or service company
- [ ] Identify founder/MD name and background
- [ ] Find any regulatory approvals or certifications
- [ ] Check copyright year on website (staleness indicator)
- [ ] Look for recent hiring or expansion news

**Output:** Detailed notes per company (can be messy at this stage)

### Step 3: Scoring & Filtering (Day 3-4)

**Goal:** Score all companies on 6 criteria, identify top 25

**Activities:**
- [ ] For ~70-80 companies that passed basic checks: score C1-C6
- [ ] Score each as: Strong (3) / Moderate (2) / Weak (1)
- [ ] Total score out of 18
- [ ] Auto-disqualify companies that fail C1, C2, or have auto-disqualify flags
- [ ] Keep companies scoring 12+ (rough cutoff for "fit" level)

**Output:** Filtered list of ~30-40 candidates

### Step 4: Deep Dive on Top 30 (Day 4)

**Goal:** Verify evidence, write personalization hooks, finalize scoring

**Activities:**
- [ ] For each top 30 company: verify each criterion with specific evidence
- [ ] Write 1-line personalization hook (specific recent detail)
- [ ] Double-check auto-disqualify flags
- [ ] Make final verdict: Strong Fit / Fit / Borderline

**Output:** Final 25 companies with evidence + hooks

### Step 5: CSV Output (Day 5)

**Goal:** Format 25 companies into submission CSV

**Fields per company:**
- Company name
- Website
- City
- Segment
- What they make (specific products)
- Revenue band (estimated)
- Decision-maker name & background
- C1-C6 scores + evidence
- Overall verdict
- Personalization hook

## What I'm Looking For in Real Companies (Examples)

### Strong Fit Pattern:
- **Website:** Modern, regular updates
- **Products:** Specific, technical (not generic "chemicals" but "specialty surfactants for pharma")
- **Founder:** PhD, IIT alumni, ex-ISRO engineer
- **Certifications:** USFDA approved, DSIR recognized, EU-GMP
- **Growth:** Recent news about expansion, new facility, hiring
- **Revenue:** Rs.100-300Cr (sweet spot for Federer)

### Fail Pattern:
- Website says "We distribute X products" = Trader, not manufacturer
- "We provide testing services" = CRO, not manufacturer
- Founder background: MBA from unknown college, no technical credentials
- No website updates since 2020
- Last press release: 2019
- Revenue data unavailable and company is tiny with no activity
