## Learnings So Far

### About Hyderabad Biotech Landscape
- Genome Valley has both strong manufacturers and service companies
- Many startups in diagnostics post-COVID
- Strong specialization in recombinant proteins and enzyme production
- Regulatory approvals (USFDA, EU-GMP) are rare but present

### Quick Screening Tips
- Website copyright year is quick indicator of staleness
- "We distribute" vs "We manufacture" matters greatly
- Founder background (PhD/IIT) highly correlates with technical differentiation
- Recent hiring posts on LinkedIn = growth signal
