# Business Analytics Research Assignment
## Target Company Research

This repository contains the Business Analytics internship assignment for DeepThought — a comprehensive research project to identify and profile ICP-qualified Indian specialty manufacturers.

### Assignment Overview

**Goal:** Find 25 target companies matching the "Federer" profile — Indian specialty manufacturers (Rs.50Cr–Rs.500Cr revenue) with technical decision-makers and evidence-based growth signals.

### Key Deliverables

1. **Part A: Company Research Output**
   - CSV with 25 profiled companies
   - Detailed methodology document
   - Code/scripts used (if applicable)

2. **Part B: Scaling Strategy**
   - Sourcing methods for finding ICP companies
   - 1000-company scale-up proposal
   - Hand-drawn diagram (submitted via Internshala)

### Evaluation Criteria

We assess on:
- Research quality and evidence discipline
- ICP judgment (differentiating commodity vs. specialty)
- Sourcing creativity
- Scale-up thinking (realistic planning)
- Communication clarity

### Timeline

- **Assignment Duration:** 5 days
- **Research Target:** 75-100 companies to find 25 that pass (~30% yield)

### About the Federer Company

Our ideal client:
- **Differentiated product** — specialty/technical, not commodity
- **Growth mindset** — investing in R&D, new facilities, capacity expansion
- **Promoter-driven** — founder/technical leader owns and builds capability in-house
- **Technical decision-maker** — founder/MD has engineering, science, or technical background
- **Active growth signals** — recent hiring, new facilities, certifications, press activity

### Key Segments (Basket A + B)

**Basket A (Strongest Fit):**
- Specialty biotech (probiotics, enzymes, recombinant proteins)
- Specialty diagnostics & life-science tools
- Custom synthesis & specialty chemicals
- Performance chemicals

**Basket B (Adjacent Specialty):**
- Complex APIs & regulated pharma
- Medical devices
- Hybrid seeds & agri-inputs
- Specialty food & nutraceuticals
- Industrial sensors & instrumentation
- Veterinary biologics

### Auto-Disqualify Flags

❌ Traders, distributors, importers
❌ Service companies (CROs, testing labs, analytical providers)
❌ Generic pharma (bulk APIs, branded generics)
❌ Subsidiaries of large groups
❌ Recently acquired by larger groups
❌ PE/VC-controlled (institutional ownership)
❌ Revenue > Rs.500Cr
❌ No website or stale website
❌ No activity in last 2 years

### Scoring Framework (6 Criteria)

| Criterion | What It Tests |
|-----------|---------------|
| C1: Manufacturer | Produces physical products in-house with real facilities |
| C2: India-based | HQ in India or significant manufacturing operations in India |
| C3: Differentiated | Technical/niche products, not commodities (patents, R&D, regulatory approvals) |
| C4: Technical DM | Founder/MD has PhD, IIT/NIT/IISc, ex-ISRO/DRDO, or engineering background |
| C5: Tailwind | Operating in sector with government/market support (PLI, Make-in-India, China+1) |
| C6: Growth Signals | Hiring, new facilities, certifications, active press, recent website updates |

Each criterion is scored: Weak / Moderate / Strong (with specific evidence)
